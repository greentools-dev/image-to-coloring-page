Coloring Page Guide & FAQ

How does the edge-detection algorithm work?

The image is first converted into 8-bit grayscale luminance. Next, a $3 \times 3$ Sobel convolution matrix calculates horizontal and vertical gradient vectors across neighboring pixels. Where luminance changes abruptly (contours, facial features, outlines), sharp dark line strokes are rendered against pure white paper.

Which photos make the best coloring pages?

Photos with good lighting, sharp focus, and high contrast between the subject and background produce the cleanest outlines. Pets, close-up portraits, architectural landmarks, and bold vehicles work exceptionally well.

Is it safe for private family photos?

Yes, 100%. Processing is executed exclusively within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas pixel manipulation. No photos or images are ever uploaded to any cloud server or third-party database.

How do I adjust line thickness?

Use the "Detail Sensitivity" slider to capture finer textures (like hair or foliage) or simplify the sketch. The "Line Darkness" slider intensifies the edge contrast for easy printing on household monochrome laser and inkjet printers.
